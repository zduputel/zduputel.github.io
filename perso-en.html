WHAT ELSE KEEPS ME BUSY… Music - In high school, I played a bit of bass guitar (1999-2001), then took up the sitar in the early 2000s. I started with Indiren Pillaï at the Conservatoire de La Réunion, then studied with Alok Lahiri around 2005-2006. After a long break, I resumed in 2023 with his son, Abhishek Lahiri. Since 2025, I have been studying with Samanwaya Sarkar. Sanskrit - I discovered Sanskrit around 2006 with Swāmī Advayānanda Sarasvatī at the Ārsha Vidyā Āshram (mainly reading and writing Devanāgarī). I took it up again in 2019 with the university diploma “Langues anciennes : sanskrit” at the University of Strasbourg, with Guillaume Ducoeur (introduction to grammar). I am now continuing with Swāmī Pranavārthānanda Sarasvatī at the Pranava Ashram (advanced grammar, pronunciation, reading texts). A few resources (in French) are on my Sanskrit page. Sports - I learned to ski at the Montgenèvre ski club (French Alps), where I was a member from 1990 to 1996. In La Réunion, I sailed (420 dinghy) at the Club nautique de Saint-Paul between 1998 and 2002. Since 2020, whenever I have a little time, I climb with a club (7A L'Ouest) or at a climbing gym (Basalte Évolution).
